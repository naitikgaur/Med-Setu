What you get

MedSetu does one thing, completely: FMGE practice, sat properly and marked honestly — previous-year papers as full mocks, subject-wise tests one subject at a time, and a question bank for daily practice. There are no courses or add-ons waiting behind it.

The real exam window

The test opens in a window modelled on the TCS iON screen the FMGE is actually held on — the same palette symbols, the same buttons in the same order, the same two-part rhythm with a break in between. On exam day, nothing about the screen is new to you.

All twelve papers, complete

December 2020 to June 2026, every paper a full 300 questions. Where a recall source itself runs short, the mock is completed with questions from other years’ papers — and every added question is flagged with the paper it came from.

Subject-wise tests

Sixteen subjects, two tests each where the series has them — 19 to 50 questions on one subject at a time, in the same exam window, every question with its full explanation. Your score is measured against the 50% mark, the FMGE pass proportion.

The Question Bank

15,607 questions across all 19 FMGE subjects, organised by topic, every one with its answer and explanation. Practise a topic at a time in sets of 20, 50 or the whole subject — the bank is where daily revision happens.

Practice mode, and mistakes that clear

In Practice mode you check each answer as you go and read the explanation straight away. Every wrong answer — in the bank or in a test — is saved to My Mistakes in this tab; replay it, answer it right, and it clears. Bookmarks work the same way.

The book’s explanations

For the ten book papers, every question carries the explanation printed in the FMGE previous-year book — word for word, not a paraphrase and not written by a machine. The two 2026 papers carry answers only, and the site says so plainly.

Click a question, read its solution

After the test, your analysis lists all 300 questions. Open any one and you see the correct option, the option you chose, and the full explanation — so revision starts from the exact questions that cost you marks.

An answer key worth downloading

The detailed answer key prints every question with the correct option, its explanation and your answer beside it — a revision document you keep, not just a score you glance at. A one-page key is there too, for a fast count.

The images are in

Image-based questions carry the actual X-ray, slide, ECG or diagram, reproduced from the book the paper was taken from. Where an image could not be recovered from the source, the question tells you instead of guessing.

See exactly where a mark went

This is a real question from the January 2024 paper, with the explanation as it is printed in the book MedSetu is built from. After a test, every one of your questions opens like this — and in the bank’s Practice mode, the explanation is there the moment you check your answer.

FMGE January 2024 · Question 115 · Community Medicine

From the book

Mid-year population is the denominator in?

  • A. CDRCorrect answer
  • B. CFR
  • C. MMR
  • D. NMR

Explanation — as printed in the book

“The mid-year population is commonly used as the denominator in rates that measure events occurring in the entire population over a year. The best example is the Crude Death Rate (CDR). The mid-year population (usually estimated on 1st July) represents the average population at risk during the year, making it the most appropriate denominator.”

The book’s note goes on to show why each of the other three options is wrong — and on MedSetu, it is all there under the question.

And around every answer, the full picture

  • Your score against the real pass mark of 150 — and the gap, stated plainly.
  • Subject-wise accuracy set against the official NBEMS blueprint, so effort goes where the marks are.
  • Time honesty: your slow questions and your rushed ones, listed separately.
  • Guesses labelled — Sure, Unsure, Guessed — so you can see what guessing actually earned you.
  • You will not be given a rank or a percentile: MedSetu has no cohort of other students to rank you against, so it does not invent one.

How it works

From this page to a marked paper in four steps — under a minute to be inside your first test.

  1. 1

    Let’s Start

    The button takes you to a short gate: your name and one quick human check. The gate creates no account, and your name stays in this tab.

  2. 2

    Pick your practice

    Choose a full previous-year paper — twelve of them, December 2020 to June 2026 — a subject-wise test on one subject at a time, or the question bank, subject by subject and topic by topic.

  3. 3

    Attempt it, or practise it

    Papers and tests open in the exam window — parts, palette, mark-for-review, the clock. In the question bank you can also choose Practice mode: check each answer and read its explanation as you go.

  4. 4

    Read the analysis

    Your score against the 150 pass mark — or the 50% benchmark for a subject test or bank set — subject by subject, every solution a click away, and a detailed answer key to download.

Built by a study hall in Gautam Nagar

MedSetu was made by the efforts of Naitik Library — a study hall for medical students in Gautam Nagar, New Delhi, founded by Naitik Gaur. Students preparing there needed previous-year papers they could sit under real exam conditions, with the book’s explanations attached. MedSetu is that, put online for every FMGE student, free.

It is built honestly: NBEMS never releases official papers, so MedSetu’s are recall-based editions and are labelled as such wherever they appear. The ten book papers carry the book’s own explanations; the two 2026 papers carry answers only, and say so. The question bank is compiled from published FMGE and entrance-exam compilations, organised by subject and topic. Nothing here is dressed up as something it is not.
