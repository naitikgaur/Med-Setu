Privacy policy

The short version: MedSetu itself collects nothing. There is no server and no database behind this site, and there are no accounts. The one exception to “nothing runs here but the site” is advertising on the homepage, explained plainly below.

Last updated: 15 November 2026

What MedSetu itself does with your information

When you use MedSetu, you type a name and attempt tests or practise questions. That name, your answers, your mistakes list and your bookmarks are stored only in your own browser tab (in what is called session storage). They are never sent to us, never written to any database, and they disappear when you close the tab.

MedSetu runs no analytics of its own and sets no cookies of its own. We cannot see who visited, which paper you attempted, or how you scored — by design, not just by promise: a static website with no server behind it has nowhere to send such information to.

Advertising on the homepage

The homepage — only the homepage — displays advertisements served by Google AdSense. The test pages, subject tests, question bank, analysis and every other page on MedSetu carry no advertising.

Google and other third-party vendors use cookies to serve ads based on a visitor's prior visits to this website and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet. Depending on your settings and choices, ads may be personalised or non-personalised.

You can read how Google uses information from sites that use its services in Google's advertising and privacy information and in the Google Privacy Policy.

You can opt out of personalised advertising at any time in Google Ads Settings, or via aboutads.info. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings — MedSetu works fully either way, and you never need an account.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page. The commitment that does not change: MedSetu itself keeps no data about its students.
